What to look for

Unknown region, consent required

The collector decides where consent is needed. If it can't tell where a visitor is, the answer is "required".

Limited means anonymous

Before consent, only essential events leave the browser: no attribution, no query strings, no identifiers.

Records are evidence

Every decision is an append-only consent record with its policy version. Withdrawal is a new record, not an edit.

Third parties wait their turn

Vendor tags load and receive events only once the visitor allows their category.
